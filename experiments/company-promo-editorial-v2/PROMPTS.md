# 생성 배경 프롬프트

세 배경은 동일한 프롬프트를 재활용하지 않고 회사별 실제 사용 맥락에 맞춰 각각 생성했다. 합성 타이포와 로고는 이미지 생성에 맡기지 않고 결정론적 SVG 렌더러에서 추가한다.

## SEVASA

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: square background photograph for a Korean B2B energy company editorial advertisement; typography and logo will be added later
Primary request: an observational photograph of a real EV charging installation inside an ordinary Korean office-building parking garage
Scene/backdrop: daytime, slightly worn concrete columns and painted floor, visible but tidy electrical conduit, practical ceiling fixtures, a little dust and everyday wear
Subject: one plain wall-mounted EV charger correctly installed on a concrete column and one unbranded compact electric car connected with a physically plausible cable; charger and car occupy the right half
Style/medium: documentary corporate reportage photography, photographed on location, candid and matter-of-fact, not staged, not stock-like, not CGI, no retouching
Composition/framing: 1:1 square, natural eye-level viewpoint, 50mm documentary lens, balanced asymmetry, deep focus; reserve the left 42 percent for later copy but keep authentic wall and floor texture rather than an empty gradient
Lighting/mood: believable mix of neutral overhead fluorescent light and soft daylight from an open side of the garage; one coherent lighting direction, restrained dynamic range, neutral white balance
Materials/textures: real concrete pores, small floor scuffs, cable weight and contact shadow, ordinary automotive paint, minor imperfections
Constraints: background only; no readable text, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, people, license-plate text, data graphics, energy trails, holograms, UI overlays, cyan glow, dramatic fog, perfect showroom styling, or watermark
Avoid: cinematic color grading, glossy 3D render, cyberpunk, teal-and-orange palette, bokeh, shallow focus, volumetric lighting, color wash, excessive symmetry, luxury-car advertising
```

## 슈퍼쇼츠

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: square background photograph for a Korean creator software advertisement; typography and logo will be added later
Primary request: a believable small creator workspace showing the ordinary tools used to turn a blog post into a short vertical video
Scene/backdrop: modest desk in a small contemporary Korean office or home studio during late morning; laptop, one smartphone on a simple stand, compact camera lens, wired mouse, notebook, charging cable and a half-finished coffee arranged naturally with slight everyday mess
Subject: laptop and smartphone occupy the right half; phone is vertical and shows one simple photographic video frame with a plain play triangle; laptop screen uses only quiet blank blocks with no readable interface text
Style/medium: candid editorial workplace photography, honest real-use context, lightly imperfect and unstaged, not product CGI, not influencer fantasy
Composition/framing: 1:1 square, eye-level seated viewpoint, 40mm lens, deep focus with only mild natural falloff; useful left-side copy space formed by a plain painted wall and the edge of the desk, not a synthetic gradient
Lighting/mood: soft window daylight with a practical desk lamp switched off, neutral color balance, normal indoor contrast, no sunset glow
Materials/textures: faint scratches on laminate desk, fingerprints on phone glass, paper edges, realistic cables and contact shadows, ordinary affordable equipment
Constraints: background only; no readable text, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, people, hands, floating screens, detached video cards, holograms, neon, impossible UI, excessive reflections, or watermark
Avoid: polished 3D render, blue-purple color wash, cinematic studio light, bokeh-heavy lens, perfect symmetry, levitating objects, futuristic interface, glossy stock-photo perfection
```

## 주식회사 로드맵

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: square background photograph for a Korean smart-parking company editorial advertisement; typography and logo will be added later
Primary request: an authentic parking facility where a real sensor installation and one available parking bay communicate smart-parking operations without science-fiction effects
Scene/backdrop: ordinary above-ground municipal or office parking structure in Anyang on an overcast afternoon, weathered concrete, practical railings, slightly worn lane markings, a small unbranded LiDAR or parking sensor correctly mounted to a column or ceiling
Subject: one unbranded compact sedan moving slowly toward the right-side bays and one clearly empty parking space; all geometry, wheel direction, shadows and reflections must be physically coherent
Style/medium: documentary architectural and infrastructure photography, objective field-report image, real working environment, no CGI or composited effects
Composition/framing: 1:1 square, natural eye-level 35mm viewpoint, deep focus, asymmetric frame; reserve the left 40 percent with a real drive aisle and concrete surface for later copy while keeping the sensor and car visible on the right
Lighting/mood: diffuse overcast daylight mixed with practical garage fixtures, restrained contrast, neutral gray color, no blue-hour drama
Materials/textures: patched concrete, faded paint, minor tire marks, realistic sensor bracket and wiring, ordinary vehicle finish
Constraints: background only; no readable text, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, people, license-plate text, glowing scan rings, point clouds, holograms, grids, UI overlays, cyan trails, impossible parking geometry, or watermark
Avoid: cinematic night city, futuristic smart-city concept art, cyberpunk, glossy render, aerial hero angle, color wash, shallow depth of field, perfect pristine architecture, dramatic volumetric light
```
