# v3 생성 배경 프롬프트

세 배경은 네이버 검색 결과에서 약 104×104px로 축소될 것을 전제로 각각 생성했다. 이미지 생성에는 글자와 로고를 맡기지 않고, 한 피사체가 중앙 80% 안에서 즉시 식별되도록 요청했다.

## SEVASA

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: square primary image for a Naver Blog search thumbnail that will be displayed as small as 104 by 104 pixels; typography will be added later
Primary request: an immediate, unmistakable close documentary photograph of an electric car actively charging in an ordinary Korean office-building parking garage
Scene/backdrop: practical daytime parking garage with worn concrete, visible conduit, painted floor and neutral fluorescent fixtures
Subject: one unbranded wall charger, its heavy cable, connector and the open charging port of one unbranded compact EV; connector, cable and charging port must be physically correct and together occupy roughly the central and right 70 percent of the square
Style/medium: candid corporate field photography, natural and matter-of-fact, not staged, not stock-like, not CGI
Composition/framing: 1:1 square, eye-level 50mm close framing; one obvious focal relationship readable at tiny thumbnail size; keep all essential objects inside the central 80 percent so both square and landscape center crops survive
Lighting/mood: believable soft daylight mixed with neutral garage lighting, one coherent light direction, restrained contrast, neutral white balance
Materials/textures: concrete pores, minor floor scuffs, cable weight, contact shadows, ordinary automotive paint
Constraints: image only; no text, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, people, hands, license-plate text, UI, infographic, glow, energy trails, holograms, watermark, split panels, or empty copy area
Avoid: cinematic color grade, cyan wash, luxury car ad, wide empty wall, glossy 3D render, shallow bokeh, perfect showroom, dramatic fog
```
