# 배경 생성 프롬프트

배경은 내장 이미지 생성 도구로 생성했다. 모든 프롬프트에서 텍스트·숫자·로고·워터마크를 금지하고 왼쪽 카피 영역을 비워 두도록 명시했다.

## SEVASA

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square company promotion background for a Korean B2B energy technology company
Primary request: an original premium campaign photograph showing a modern indoor EV charging area integrated with intelligent building energy management
Scene/backdrop: contemporary Korean commercial building parking facility, clean concrete architecture, one elegant electric vehicle connected to a compact wall charger
Subject: the EV and charger occupy the right half; subtle luminous energy-flow lines and a restrained data-grid glow suggest AI power prediction and control
Style/medium: photorealistic commercial technology photography with understated editorial polish, credible real-world infrastructure
Composition/framing: square 1:1, eye-level wide view, strong usable negative space on the left and upper-left for later headline placement, uncluttered foreground
Lighting/mood: deep navy ambient light with crisp cool cyan highlights, trustworthy, sustainable, technically sophisticated
Color palette: midnight navy, electric cyan, cool white, a very small warm amber accent
Constraints: background image only; no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand marks, no watermark; do not place important objects in the left 42 percent; charger label blank; realistic cable and parking geometry
Avoid: sci-fi spaceship aesthetic, neon overload, distorted car, illegible signage, fake UI panels, people
```

## 슈퍼쇼츠

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square promotional background for a Korean AI short-form video creation service
Primary request: an original energetic but premium campaign scene showing a modern creator desk and a large upright smartphone presenting a vivid vertical video editing experience
Scene/backdrop: clean contemporary studio desk at dusk, subtle camera gear and notebook shapes in soft focus, floating rectangular video frames and timeline fragments suggesting one-click transformation from blog content into short video
Subject: smartphone and dynamic video-frame stack occupy the right half; phone display contains only abstract image blocks, playhead shapes and a clear play triangle, never readable text
Style/medium: polished commercial 3D-photographic hybrid, believable materials, youthful Korean tech-product advertising, not a UI screenshot
Composition/framing: square 1:1, three-quarter close view, generous clean negative space on the left 44 percent for later headline placement, strong diagonal motion toward the phone
Lighting/mood: bright, fast, optimistic, creator-friendly
Color palette: SuperShorts-inspired electric blue #388DFF, violet, soft pink highlight, deep indigo, clean white
Materials/textures: glossy glass phone, soft matte desk, subtle paper texture
Constraints: background image only; no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark; no social media brand icons; leave left side visually quiet; one phone only; realistic hand-free product scene
Avoid: clutter, cyberpunk darkness, fake unreadable interface text, excessive neon, distorted phone, generic corporate people
```

## 주식회사 로드맵

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square promotional background for a Korean smart-parking and mobility technology company
Primary request: an original premium campaign image of an intelligent urban parking system using AI and LiDAR to find and guide vehicles into available spaces
Scene/backdrop: modern multi-level city parking facility at blue hour, clear parking geometry, subtle view toward a Korean urban skyline
Subject: a single modern car moving into a parking bay on the lower-right; elegant cyan LiDAR scan arcs, precise point-cloud traces and one highlighted empty parking space communicate sensing and optimization
Style/medium: photorealistic architectural mobility advertising, clean engineering visualization integrated naturally into the photograph
Composition/framing: square 1:1, elevated three-quarter perspective, dark structured environment, generous quiet negative space across upper-left and center-left for later typography
Lighting/mood: confident, precise, urban, future-ready but believable
Color palette: Roadmap-inspired deep navy #0A1A26, bright cyan #79EBFF, white, restrained teal
Materials/textures: concrete, painted lane markings, glass, realistic automotive reflections
Constraints: background image only; no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark; no visible license plate text; no people; keep left 42 percent low-detail; exactly one highlighted empty parking bay
Avoid: futuristic flying cars, cyberpunk clutter, excessive holograms, distorted parking lines, fake signs, unreadable UI text
```
